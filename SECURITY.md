# Security & Data Privacy Policy

## Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

AmiCare is designed solely as a non-diagnostic stress self-assessment tool and support resource finder. It is **not** a replacement for professional clinical care, psychiatric diagnosis, or emergency mental health crisis intervention.

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a medical crisis, please immediately contact local emergency services or call a 24/7 helpline:
- **Tele-MANAS (India)**: 14416 or 1800-891-4416
- **KIRAN Helpline**: 1800-599-0019
- **National Emergency**: 112

## Privacy & Data Protection Commitments

1. **Zero PII Collection**: AmiCare does not require user registration, real names, email addresses, or phone numbers to use the self-assessment tool or helpline directory.
2. **Local Session Persistence**: Self-assessment scores and progress tracking reside locally in browser memory (`localStorage`) and are not transmitted to persistent user-identifiable backend logs.
3. **Encrypted Communication**: All API communication occurs over standard HTTPS in production environments.
4. **No Third-Party Tracking**: AmiCare contains zero third-party advertising scripts or tracking pixels.

## Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

We take security and privacy seriously. If you discover a vulnerability or security issue:
- Do **not** open a public GitHub issue.
- Report disclosures to `security@amicare.org` with details of the finding.
- We aim to acknowledge receipt within 24 hours and issue a patch within 7 business days.
